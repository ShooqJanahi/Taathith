Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about furniture browsing, room measurement, fit validation, sellers, and ordering.

  • What is Taathith?

    Taathith is a home décor and furniture web app that connects users with local furniture sellers and helps users visualize furniture in their space before buying.

  • Can I check if furniture fits my room?

    Yes, users can en2ter room dimensions or upload a room image, then use the fit validation feature to check whether the selected furniture is suitable.

  • Can sellers add their products?

    Yes, sellers can register, upload furniture details and images, manage product availability, and receive orders through the platform.
